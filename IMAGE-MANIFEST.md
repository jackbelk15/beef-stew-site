## Acquisition requirements

- Prefer original/licensed images from Adobe Stock, Getty, Shutterstock, Unsplash, or Pexels. Record the source URL and license with the downloaded asset.
- Export sRGB WebP. Use the target dimensions above, quality 75–85, and keep each image visually legible at its crop.
- Avoid visible third-party logos, text, watermarks, uncanny hands/faces, and images that imply a real food-safety certification.
- Keep filenames unchanged so no HTML or CSS edits are needed. The six supplied files have been assigned above and their old placeholder slots are crossed off. After replacement, inspect the homepage at desktop and mobile widths and update the relevant `alt` text to describe the final image rather than calling it a placeholder.
