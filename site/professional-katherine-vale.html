Professional profile

Katherine Vale

Managing Partner | Whole-System Strategy

Katherine helps organizations decide where the stew system should be deliberately complex and where it has simply accumulated habits.

→ katherine.vale@beef-stew.com

Her practice

Enterprise decisions with a clear route to the bowl.

Katherine is brought in when stew has become an executive agenda item but not yet an executable agenda. She helps leaders align investment, operations, supply, and commercial ambition around a system that has to work in practice as well as in a board book.

Her work starts with the places where a useful answer is hard to find: across business units, between an annual plan and the conditions in the field, or inside a portfolio that has become more numerous than strategic. Katherine gives teams a shared fact base, a point of view, and an appropriate number of decisions.

Clients rely on her for judgment that is broad enough to connect the whole lifecycle and specific enough to clarify who changes what on Monday morning. She does not make stew complicated. She does ask why it already is.
