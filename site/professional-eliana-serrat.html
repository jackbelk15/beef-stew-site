Dr. Eliana Serrat

Partner | Broth Systems and Material Quality

Eliana connects food science to operating decisions when material behavior, quality expectations, and management reporting no longer agree.

→ eliana.serrat@beef-stew.com

Her practice

Material reality is still part of the strategy.

Eliana works where the physical behavior of a stew system becomes too consequential to be translated loosely. She helps clients understand the relationship between raw-material condition, process choices, broth development, and the quality promise made beyond the production floor.

Her perspective is scientific, but the work is firmly operational. Eliana gives leadership teams an intelligible view of what the materials are doing, why it matters, and which intervention is worth making before a quality issue is renamed a commercial issue.

Clients value her ability to make complex technical conditions useful to people who need to fund, govern, or explain them. In her work, rigor is not an add-on. It is the basis for a more defensible stew position.
