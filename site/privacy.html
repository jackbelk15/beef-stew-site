Effective September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Beef-Stew Co. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with beef-stew.com and pages that we operate under that domain (the “Site”).

Purchases. If you purchase merchandise, payment information is collected and processed by Stripe through a Stripe-hosted payment page. We do not intend to receive or store your full payment-card number on the Site. Stripe’s handling of your information is governed by Stripe’s own privacy terms.

Data retention and security

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information that we control. We may retain information when required or permitted for legal, security, accounting, or operational reasons.

Contact
