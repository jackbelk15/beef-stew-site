Her practice

Supply resilience that reaches the commercial decision.

Priya advises clients at the point where ingredient volatility stops being a procurement detail and starts influencing margin, customer promises, and the credibility of the plan. She builds the bridge between what the field is doing and what leadership needs to decide.

Her work combines supplier strategy, scenario design, demand priorities, and price architecture. The objective is not to make input variance disappear, which would be magical. It is to make the organization materially more prepared for it.

Clients work with Priya when they need an operating response that protects the bowl without treating every potato as a strategic emergency. She brings discipline to alternate supply paths, commercial trade-offs, and the conditions under which a contingency becomes necessary.
