Professional profile

Miles Harrow

Principal | Governance and Operating Model

Miles gives responsibility, escalation, and decision rights to the parts of stew operations that have acquired several owners and no mandate.

→ miles.harrow@beef-stew.com

His practice

When the ladle belongs to everyone, it belongs to no one.

Miles works with organizations where the operating model has become very collaborative and therefore unable to decide what to do with the broth. He establishes the roles, measures, routines, and thresholds that let teams intervene before a small condition becomes a material discussion.

His engagements expose the hidden handoffs between procurement, production, finance, quality, and leadership. Then he creates a practical model for decision-making that is detailed enough to be useful and concise enough for people to follow when the vessel is hot.

Miles is valued for his ability to turn ambiguous responsibility into accountable work. Clients leave with fewer committees, clearer mandates, and a defined answer to the surprisingly persistent question of who may alter the broth.
